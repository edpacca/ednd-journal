#### Role
- Tavern Keeper
- Map maker
- Ex-Sailor
#### Characteristics
- Talks real slow-like, thick drawl,
- Grunts often. Gravelly cough
- Loves a good story and likes those who take risks
- Scarred and chipped shell, grizzly but cheerful looking
- Doesn't wear clothes
- Has a faint symbol engraved into the shell on the back
#### Notes
- If asked if Hal is the Drunken Tortle, he replied "Sometimes.."
- Hal has been on Murraka for nearly 60 years, since the old days. Remembers a lot. 
- Was a bit of a bucaneer and trader, deck hand, galavanter. Eventually decided to settle down and won the building which is now the Drunken Tortle in a wild bet.
#### Wants
- Prosperity for the bar
- interesting stories
- New locations for his maps
- his lost stash
#### Fears
- Rivermine being overrun by another organisation
- Being forced to leave Murraka
- Doesn't trust magic users too much
